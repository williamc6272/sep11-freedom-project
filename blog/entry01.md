## Content
For my final freedom project, I decided to focus on math and an alarm. I chose this topic because I want to create something that helps people feel more refreshed and ready to start their mornings. My idea is to combine math questions with an alarm so people can wake up their brains by solving simple problems. I am also going to use Howler.js to add sound effects and alarm sounds to my project. Howler.js will allow me to control when the alarm plays and when it stops. I will use the JavaScript skills I have learned in class, including basics, conditionals, functions, and arrays, to make everything work together. In my learning log, I explored Howler.js and learned how it can play audio files using JavaScript. I practiced using `play()`, `pause()`, and `stop()` to control sounds. I also explored .volume(0.5) and learned that volume can be adjusted from 0 to 1. I looked into Howler Audio Sprites and how different sounds can be played from one audio file. I started thinking about how I could use different notification sounds when the timer goes off and possibly allow users to choose their own sounds. Next, I plan on exploring the Howler.js documentation and experimenting with its different features so I can better understand how to use it in my final project.
